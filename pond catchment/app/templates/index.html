How it works

1. Interpolate DEM: Converts discrete contour isolines into a continuous elevation grid using SciPy's spatial interpolators.

2. D8 Flow Routing: Calculates the direction of water flow for each grid cell based on the local steepest descent.

3. Sink Tracing: Identifies closed basins (sinks) and groups all upstream cells flowing into them to compute the catchment boundaries.
